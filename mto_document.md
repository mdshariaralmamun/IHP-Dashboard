## Calculation Rules Applied

### Pipe Length
- Calculate based on distance from tie-in point to equipment
- Above ceiling run: 18 L.M. (horizontal along X-axis)
- Below ceiling run: 3 L.M. (vertical down along Z-axis)
- **Total Pipe Length:** 21 L.M.

### Fitting Quantities
- **90° Elbows:** 4 EA (specified in project requirements)
- **45° Elbows:** 5 EA (specified in project requirements)
- **Couplings:** (Pipe length / 6m) - 1 = (21/6) - 1 = 3.5 - 1 ≈ 2 EA (rounded up)
- **Supports:** 1 per 3m of pipe run = 21/3 = 7 supports (minimum), provided as per Stauff Clamps

### Valve and Fitting Counts
- **Ball Valves:** 2 EA (isolation at tie-in and POU regulator)
- **Dielectric Unions:** 1 EA (copper to SS transition)
- **Reducers:** 1 EA (if size transition required)
- **Swagelok Connectors:** 3 EA (male 1/2"x1/4", male 1/2"x1/2", and regulator connection)

### Insulation Quantities
- **Above Ceiling (Copper):** 21 L.M. (total pipe run)
- **Below Ceiling (SS):** 21 L.M. (total pipe run, same length)
- **Fitting Insulation:** Additional 15% for elbows and fittings

### Labeling
- N2 Labels: Yellow background, Black text "NITROGEN"
- Quantity: 1 LOT (covers all pipe runs and fittings)
