QUERY DESCRIPTION 
During the Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) test conducted at the location of the 
concrete foundation, existing underground utilities were identified within the foundation 
footprint. Proceeding with the foundation construction at the current location poses a 
risk of damaging these utilities and may cause service  disruptions and safety concerns.
We propose to to relocate the concrete foundation to a suitable loca tion clear of the 
identified utilities. A new location and coordination's were provided for consideration.
