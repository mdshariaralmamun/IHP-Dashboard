EAR# 12518 
Rev.-1 
 
CT X-ray room modification (ASEPC) 
Building  3 Level 0 Area 3 Rm#3-0132 
 
 
1. Introduction 
• King Abdullah University of Science & Technology (KAUST) intends to avail the 
services CT X-ray room modification (ASEPC) at Building  3 Level 0 Area 3 Rm#3-
0132. 
2. Scope of Work 
 Civil/Architectural 
• Works under this section include but not limited to modification of the existing lab 
caseworks, reworks, repairs, and repainting affected wall to match existing finish. Any 
penetration on the fire rated wall, to be sealed with fire rated sealant. 
Ceiling works: 
• Supply and install acoustic ceiling tiles (Armstrong or any equivalent) 600mm x 
600mmx 16mm with wet format mineral fiber, washable paint finish, white color, 75% 
light reflectance or more, square-cut lay-in trimmed edges, NRC range 0.5-0.6, STC 
range 35-39, surface burning class A per ASTM E1264 and fed. Spec. SS-S-
characteristics 118B. Flame spread 25 or under UL labeled, with suspension system T 
bar system, including all necessary galvanized steel main runners, cross runners, 
hangers, U-shape slots/ UNISTRUT channels, splice plate, and all required 
accessories, or any available and approved from KAUST. 
 
Electrical 
Power outlets: 
• Relocate 2 duplex socket no. UN2400-PR-009-CKT-04 to be behind the PC. 
• Supply and install 12 AWG - THHN/THWN wires as required.  
• Supply and install Belden-9841and Belden-5341fe for the VAVs and the thermostats. 
• Supply and install 1-inch EMT conduit with required accessories. 
• Supply and install 1-inch flexible EMT conduit with required accessories. 
• Testing & commissioning (Continuity/Insulation Test, Volt metering Test), tagging for 
all electrical points (for cables & color coding/conduits/panel board &conduits 
grounding) shall be provided with Panel board updated termination schedule as per 
KAUST procedure.

Low Current / Telecommunication 
• (Not Seen)

HVAC 
• Relocate the existing VAV box (11-19), including all necessary modifications and 
extensions to the associated ductwork as per drawings . 
• Relocate the existing VAV room thermostat to the CT X-Ray Room (011-019). 
• Supply and install GI circular ductwork (Ø300 mm to Ø500 mm), complete with all 
fittings, supports, hangers, sealants, and accessories required for a complete 
installation. 
• Supply and install of fiberglass insulation 24k.g/m3 , 50mm thickness  
• Supply and install six (6) ceiling-mounted supply air diffusers, size 600 × 600 mm, 
complete with volume control dampers (VCDs) and plenum box . 
• Supply and install three (3) ceiling-mounted return air diffusers, size 600 × 600 mm. 
• Supply and install four (4) additional volume control dampers (VCDs) as required for 
air balancing. 
• Supply and install two (2) transfer grilles, size 400 × 400 mm, complete with all 
required accessories. 
• Modify, configure, and integrate the HVAC control system with the existing IKAS 
Building system  
• Perform Testing, Adjusting, and Balancing (TAB) to achieve the specified airflow 
rates and design conditions.

Fire Sprinkler System 
• Supply and install new fire sprinkler system to be installed to accommodate new 
ceiling and wall configurations per the construction documents. 
• Supply and install new piping to be added for new sprinkler system as necessary fire 
sprinkler heads to accommodate modifications adequate fire protection as per 
standard. 
• Supply and install fire sprinkler heads to be located per NFPA 13 for Ordinary Hazard 
Occupancy. Fire sprinkler heads are not to exceed 130 Sq. Ft. (12.1 sqm); maximum 
distance between sprinklers is 15' (4.5m); minimum distance between sprinklers is 6' 
(1.8m); maximum distance of any sprinkler from a wall is 7'6" (2.3m). 
• Supply and install of Victaulic Style 920 Mechanical Tee, painted ductile iron body, 
100mm run x 50mm female threaded outlet with EPDM gasket. Core hole and install 
100mm x 50mm female threaded Victaulic Style 920 mechanical tee on the 100mm 
wet sprinkler main pipeline. 
• Prepare shop drawings in accordance with NFPA 13 and the information provided as 
part of this scope and accompanying drawing. Shop drawings to show reflected ceiling 
plan, dimensions, pipe sizes and hanger locations. Provide sprinkler legend, hanger 
details and pipe schedule to adequately portray work being done as part of this 
remodel. 
• Supply and installation of wet pipe sprinkler system (automatic sprinkler system) with 
threaded ends, standard weight steel pipe for 50mm dia. and smaller and grooved ends 
for pipes larger than 50mm dia. ASTM A 53/A 53M, ASTM A 135, ASTM A 795 
including fittings and pipe supports. Work shall also include application of paint in 
accordance with International Color Standards. For other work, refer to the related 
specifications. 
3. General, Codes, and Standards 
• All work shall be carried out in accordance with applicable standard and comply with 
KAUST standard/specification. 
• The work shall comply with the latest edition of the international codes and standards 
as a minimum requirement. 
• All work shall comply with KAUST Work Permit and other HSE related policies and 
procedures. 
• All work shall follow KAUST quality procedures,
