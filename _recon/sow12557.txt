CT X-ray room modification (ASEPC) 
Building  3 Level 0 Area 3 Rm#3-0132 
 
 
1. Introduction 
• King Abdullah University of Science & Technology (KAUST) intends to avail the 
services CT X-ray room modification (ASEPC) at Building  3 Level 0 Area 3 Rm#3-
0132. 
2. Scope of Work 
 Civil/Architectural 
• Works under this section include but not limited to modification of the existing lab 
caseworks, reworks, repairs, and repainting affected wall to match existing finish. Any 
penetration on the fire rated wall, to be sealed with fire rated sealant. 
Ceiling works: 
• Supply and install acoustic ceiling tiles (Armstrong or any equivalent) 600mm x 
600mmx 16mm with wet format mineral fiber, washable paint finish, white color, 75% 
light reflectance or more, square-cut lay-in trimmed edges, NRC range 0.5-0.6, STC 
range 35-39, surface burning class A per ASTM E1264 and fed. Spec. SS-S-
characteristics 118B. Flame spread 25 or under UL labeled, with suspension system T 
bar system, including all necessary galvanized steel main runners, cross runners, 
hangers, U-shape slots/ UNISTRUT channels, splice plate, and all required 
accessories, or any available and approved from KAUST. 
 
Electrical 
Power outlets: 
• Relocate 2 duplex socket no. UN2400-PR-009-CKT-04 to be behind the PC. 
• Supply and install 12 AWG - THHN/THWN wires as required.  
• Supply and install Belden-9841and Belden-5341fe for the VAVs and the thermostats. 
• Supply and install 1-inch EMT conduit with required accessories. 
• Supply and install 1-inch flexible EMT conduit with required accessories. 
• Testing & commissioning (Continuity/Insulation Test, Volt metering Test), tagging for 
all electrical points (for cables & color coding/conduits/panel board &conduits 
grounding) shall be provided with Panel board updated termination schedule as per 
KAUST procedure.
