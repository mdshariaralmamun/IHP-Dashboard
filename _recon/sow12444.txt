1. Introduction 
• King Abdullah University of Science & Technology (KAUST) intends to avail the 
services Installation of additional shading within the turtle clinic. at Building 26 & 27 
Seaside area. 
2. Scope of Work 
 Civil/Architectural 
• Works under this section include, but are not limited to, the modification of the 
existing Turtle Clinic area, including all associated alterations and the repair of 
affected surfaces to match the existing finishes. Any penetrations through the concrete 
floor slab shall be properly repaired and reinstated in accordance with KAUST 
standards. 
• Design, supply, and installation of an additional 230 m² shading system within the 
Turtle Clinic area with height of 4180mm, as indicated on the drawings, including the 
complete steel support structure, PVC-coated polyester tensile membrane, all 
associated materials, fittings, anchorage systems, utility detection/scanning services 
prior to any excavation, drilling, or concrete cutting works, and all installation and 
commissioning works required for a complete and fully functional system. 
o The design scope shall include a complete structural analysis and the 
submission of a comprehensive structural design package, including but not 
limited to: 
- Structural design calculation sheets. 
- Three-dimensional structural model of the steel support system. 
- Design load assumptions and load combinations in accordance with the related 
codes (SBC) and KAUST standards. 
- Analysis of lateral deflections, internal forces (including shear forces and 
bending moments), and overall structural stability. 
- Design and verification of all steel connections, base plates, anchor bolts, and 
fixings. 
- Calculation of column reactions.
