## Pricing Logic Applied

### Unit Price Source
- **Historical Database:** Referenced KAUST internal pricing database (last updated Q2 2026)
- **Market Rates:** Current supplier quotes from approved vendors (SABIC Industrial, Al Marami Trading)
- **Adjustment:** 5% contingency applied to long-lead items (regulator, valves)

### Calculation Rules
- **Total = Qty × Unit Price** (for all line items)
- **Section Subtotals:** Sum of all line item totals within each section
- **Sub-Total:** Sum of all section subtotals (11,087.00 SAR)
- **VAT 15%:** Sub-Total × 0.15 = 1,663.05 SAR
- **Total + VAT:** Sub-Total + VAT = 12,750.05 SAR
- **USD Conversion:** Total + VAT ÷ 3.75 = 3,400.28 USD (fixed KAUST exchange rate)
