## 1. Introduction

This Scope of Work (SOW) defines the engineering, procurement, and construction activities for the N2 (Nitrogen) line installation project at KAUST. The project encompasses the installation of a new nitrogen distribution system to support laboratory and research facilities within the specified building area.

**Project Intent:**
- Provide a reliable, safe, and compliant nitrogen supply network
- Replace aging infrastructure with modern, code-compliant piping
- Integrate with existing utility distribution systems
- Minimize disruption to ongoing research operations

**Project Scope Boundary:**
- Scope includes piping, valves, instrumentation, and minor structural modifications
- Excludes civil foundation work outside designated area
- Excludes long-term maintenance contract obligations

---

## 2. Scope of Work – Detailed Breakdown by Trade

### 2.1 Civil/Structural
- Review existing floor conditions and capacity
- Cut and patch floor openings for pipe penetrations
- Install pipe supports and hangers per KAUST standard drawings
- Minor concrete patching at penetration locations

### 2.2 Mechanical (Piping)
- Supply and install N2 medium-pressure piping system
- All fittings, valves, and appurtenances
- Piping routing as per approved P&ID diagrams
- Thermal expansion compensation with expansion joints
- Valve installation (isolation, control, and safety valves)

### 2.3 Electrical
- Power supply for motor-driven valves and instrumentation
- Control wiring for automatic valve actuation
- Emergency shutdown system integration
- Instrument air supply connections (if applicable)

### 2.4 Instrumentation
- Flow meter installation and calibration
- Pressure transmitter installation
- Temperature monitoring points
- Alarm and trip system integration

### 2.5 Safety and Compliance
- N2 purge and safety procedures
- Ventilation requirements for nitrogen displacement
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) requirements
- Emergency response protocols

## 5. Exclusions

The following items are expressly excluded from this Scope of Work:

- Civil site work beyond immediate pipe penetration area
- Structural steel modifications outside piping support locations
- Permanent crane rental or heavy lifting equipment
- Ongoing maintenance contract services
- Training of operations staff (arranged separately)
- Utility connection fees to external reticulation systems
- Relocation of existing equipment not directly associated with N2 line
- Demolition of non-utility structures
